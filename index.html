Hush Alchemy Uploader

Hush Alchemy makes late-night R&B and deep house music for its YouTube channel, @hush.alchemy.

Hush Alchemy Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish times, and read the channel's own video status. It is not offered to other users.
