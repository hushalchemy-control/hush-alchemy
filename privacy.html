Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Hush Alchemy Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Hush Alchemy (@hush.alchemy). It is used only by that owner, on that owner's own channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google's sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos and thumbnails, set their titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and scheduled publish times, and read the status of the channel's own videos.

What the app stores

The app stores only the authorisation token Google issues, on the owner's own computer, so that uploads can run on schedule. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access any other person's account or data.

Third parties

Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube through the YouTube API Services. Use of the app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the Hush Alchemy YouTube channel's About page.
